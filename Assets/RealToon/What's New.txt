What's New to RealToon V5.0.16 (September 2026)
=====================================

[New]
- Refraction (URP and HDRP) [Unity]
*To use this you need to enable Transparent Mode.

- Eye Parallax Refraction (URP and HDRP) [Unity]
*Parallax style refraction for eye.
*Can also be affected by Normal Map.

- Use UV1 (URP and HDRP) [Unity]
*Force use UV1.
*New option for SDF (ShadowT) and Anisotropic Mode (Gloss Texture).

- Early added Unity 6.7 support. (URP) [Unity]
*It includes Surface Cache GI and Screen Space Reflection.
*Some improvements and changes, just for Unity 6.7.

- Enable Surface Cache GI. (New Option for Lighting) (URP) [Unity 6.7]
*For this to work you need to add Surface Cache GI in your renderer settings.

- Enable RealTime Emission. (New Option for Lighting) (URP) [Unity 6.7]
*For this to work you need to add Screen Space Reflection in your renderer settings.

[Improvements]
- Outline Width Vertex Color Blue, Smooth Object Normal Vertex Color Red, Self Shadow Threshold Vertex Color Green. (URP and HDRP) [Unity]
*100% follows RGB Vertex Colors.

[Fixed]
- DOTS Deformation variable casting error. (URP) [Unity]
- Some checkbox turns to textbox. (Unity 6.0 to Later) [Unity]
